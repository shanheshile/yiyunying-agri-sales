# Reporting

## Daily customer action list

List each material customer by verified name, country, current stage, latest
intent, confirmed product/use/quantity/address, current blocker, action completed,
verification result, next owner and due time. Highlight freight inquiry, product
quote, material send, future purchase timing, concern, explicit refusal, payment,
balance, production, shipping and after-sales states.

For customers ready for freight inquiry, output the exact freight template fields
and missing values. For customers needing formal price/PI, report the calculation
source, currency, term, status and confirmation owner without exposing internal
cost in a customer-facing draft.

## Daily totals

At minimum report:

- inquiry count and new inquiries handled;
- WhatsApp unique customers, messages, sent, delivered, read and real replies;
- other lead-source assigned/reached/replied counts when configured;
- old customers contacted and outcome groups;
- email sent, read, replied, bounced/failed;
- CRM writes and verified stage changes;
- public-pool reviews, moves and explicit-refusal moves;
- quotation/freight/PI confirmation queues;
- account/channel status, restrictions and next actions.

Use only verified action-ledger counts. Unknown data is `未核验`, not zero.
