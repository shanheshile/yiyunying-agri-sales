# Distribution Runbook

## First deployment

Configure salesperson external name, WhatsApp, email, company/legal seller,
product-line website mapping, timezone, work start, product/catalog source,
material sources, quote builder, profit-field semantics, CRM adapter, channel
adapters, confirmation policy and report fields. Keep credentials outside Git.

## Per-customer run

1. Bind and audit the full customer evidence.
2. Check the one-time background-research result.
3. Classify latest intent, product line, real stage, protection and refusal state.
4. Resolve the exact product/use and only the minimum missing fact.
5. If price/material/freight/PI is needed, load the dedicated source and skill.
6. Draft customer-language WhatsApp or a complete email. Do not batch customers; finish and verify one before the next.
7. Apply the active permission mode and confirmation boundary.
8. Verify channel result, write concise CRM facts, set supported stage and read both back.
9. Add the customer to the daily action list and ledger.

## Fixed-answer boundary

A deployment may pre-approve low-risk factual answers such as a current standard
production estimate only when the exact product/source and non-guaranteed wording
are configured. Product-specific lead time, inventory, tax, certificate, shipping
arrival and DDP scope are never inferred from a generic answer.

## Tool degradation

If a live catalog, material page, CRM, WhatsApp or email cannot be read reliably,
continue only with evidence that remains complete. Produce drafts or a blocked
queue; do not simulate sends, writes or quotes. Authentication/session failures
belong in the exception report, not customer CRM dynamics.
