# No Auto Follow

This mode changes permission only. It does not change product facts, evidence
requirements or safety boundaries.

- Allow user-requested evidence reading, customer analysis, drafts, internal calculations, action queues and CRM suggestions.
- Do not independently patrol inboxes, CRM or chat lists.
- Do not send WhatsApp/email, contact freight forwarders, write CRM, change stage, move/archive customers, issue quotation/PI/payment details or enable a schedule.
- For a direct user-requested real action, show the exact action and obtain confirmation at the action boundary unless the current task explicitly and narrowly authorizes it.
- Never report a draft as sent or a suggested stage as saved.
