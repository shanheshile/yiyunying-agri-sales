# Qualification And Materials

## Minimum qualification

For tractors, confirm horsepower range, 2WD/4WD, cab/open station, emission or
registration need, primary work, terrain, row/width constraints, PTO/hydraulics,
implements, loader/attachment capacity and quantity.

For remote mowers, confirm cutting width, area, slope and whether it is degrees
or percent, longitudinal/side slope, terrain, access width, grass/brush type,
maximum stem diameter/height, shredding expectation, engine/emission need and
quantity. Convert slope percent to degrees only with a stated formula; do not
present a catalog climbing angle as proof for every direction and surface.

For implements and attachments, confirm tractor horsepower, three-point category,
PTO speed/spline, hydraulic flow/pressure, mounting interface, working width,
load/capacity, quantity and transport configuration.

For excavators or other product lines, use only a configured current catalog and
collect operating weight, digging depth/reach, bucket/attachment, engine/emission,
track/width constraints, quantity and destination. Do not reuse mower or tractor
facts.

## Recommendation boundary

Recommend only models whose verified capability covers the customer's work.
When vegetation, slope, lifting load, access width or registration exceeds the
known envelope, report the missing proof and ask for the smallest deciding fact.
Do not infer heavy forestry capability from a lawn video or a model family name.

## Images, videos and public pages

Material priority is exact current model and configuration, exact series with the
difference stated, then a generic category image only when explicitly labelled as
illustrative. Verify that the link/file opens and matches the model before send.

Use a company or product website only when it supports the current decision,
such as first identity verification, an explicit catalogue request or an exact
product page. Do not append the same website to every message. Brand and website
routing come from the deployment profile, never a placeholder.

Record the material ID/link, match grade, language, verified time and actual send
result. Never claim an attachment or video was sent without channel read-back.
