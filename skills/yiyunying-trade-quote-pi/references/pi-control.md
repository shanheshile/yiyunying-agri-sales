# PI Control

## Before creation

Verify exact buyer legal name/address, seller legal entity, quote and order
references, product ID/model/configuration, quantity, unit, currency, unit/total
price, incoterm with named destination, packing, payment terms, bank beneficiary,
account, bank, SWIFT, validity, lead-time wording and signature/seal requirement.

Use only verified buyer and bank data. Do not infer legal identity from a chat
display name and never reuse bank details from an unrelated PI.

## Cross-check

- Sum each line and the total using decimal arithmetic.
- Match currency and term to the approved quotation.
- Match product/configuration and attachments to the CRM/order.
- Match packing to current product evidence and freight basis.
- Record deposit and balance requirements separately. A promised payment is not receipt.

## Sending and stage

A generated PI remains a draft. Present the exact customer, file/version,
amount, currency, term, bank and deviations for human confirmation. After sending,
read back recipient, attachment and message status before setting B3.

Move to A only after verified prepayment receipt. Continue recording expected
balance, due point and actual receipt until fully paid.

## Payment and high-risk documents

Do not promise cash on delivery or unverified platform protection. Payment method,
tax document, certificate, brand authorization, dangerous-goods document and
customs scope require current deployment evidence and approval.
