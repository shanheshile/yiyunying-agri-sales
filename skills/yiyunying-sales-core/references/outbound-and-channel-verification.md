# Outbound And Channel Verification

## WhatsApp

- Use the customer's language without an external Chinese translation.
- For a customer who genuinely needs a reply, normally send two to four substantive messages. Put each complete sentence in its own message.
- The first message acknowledges the exact intent or feedback. The second provides a verified fact or asks one minimum question that decides the next step.
- Two messages are not a quota: remain silent for pure thanks with no open promise or next action.
- Do not repeat a website, company introduction, catalogue or generic greeting in every conversation.
- Never send an unrelated image/video or use a material as proof of an unverified capability.

## Email

Every customer email must have a non-empty, customer-specific body in the
customer's language. Include the concrete need/context, verified product/use or
other useful fact, one minimum next step, and a complete configured signature.
Do not include a Chinese translation in the actual send.

Use a distinct, relevant subject and substantively different body for each
customer. Detect empty bodies, placeholders, duplicate/similar subjects, wrong
recipient, wrong language, stale attachments and invalid signature fields before
send. A failed email must be corrected and re-audited before retrying; do not
replay the same content rapidly.

## Channel selection

Use the customer's working preferred channel. If WhatsApp is unavailable, use a
verified email. If email fails, record the failure reason and avoid repeated
retry. Do not send highly similar content through both channels in a short period
unless the first channel failed, the customer requested both, or a verified urgent
purchase action requires it.

## Preflight

Check customer binding, real name, country, language, product line, latest
inbound, last outbound, protection time, open promise, required facts, material
match, high-risk claims, channel health, subject/body similarity and daily cap.

## Postflight

Read the actual recipient, timestamp, full text, attachments and final status.
Count a WhatsApp customer as delivered only after final delivered/read evidence;
keep sent and delivered counts separate. Count an email only after it appears in
Sent with the intended recipient, subject, non-empty body and attachments. Drafts,
input-box residue, clicks and toasts are not sends.
