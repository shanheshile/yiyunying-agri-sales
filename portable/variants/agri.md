# Agricultural Variant

- Qualify category, use, area/capacity, terrain, material/vegetation, width, power, emission/registration, quantity, attachments and destination.
- Resolve exact stable product ID/model/configuration from the current catalog before using parameters, packing, price or materials.
- Effective-dated exact-product overrides outrank live detail; live detail outranks approved snapshot; historical chat/quotes are recheck leads only.
- Product images/videos must match the exact model or be clearly labeled representative.
- Gross weight below machine/net weight is a hard freight conflict. Keep reported and calculated CBM separate; a difference over 10% blocks booking until confirmed.
- Changed packing invalidates freight based on the old packing.
