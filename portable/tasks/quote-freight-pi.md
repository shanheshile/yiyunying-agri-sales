# Quote, Freight And PI Task

- Confirm customer/country, exact product/configuration, quantity, current cost authority, verified image, packing, destination, incoterm, currency, exchange-rate source/time and included costs.
- EXW may be prepared without freight only when product and cost are verified; state exclusions.
- Declare markup-on-cost versus gross-margin-on-sale. Do not mix currencies or apply margin twice.
- For CIF/CFR/CNF confirm port and CBM. For DAP/DDU/DDP confirm address/postcode, gross weight, unloading and tax/customs scope.
- Keep each forwarder option complete and independent. Compare only matching customer, packing, destination, term and scope.
- A lower internal cost never automatically lowers an existing customer quote.
- A PI must match the approved quote, buyer/seller, product/configuration, quantity, currency, term/destination, totals, payment, bank data, validity and signature. No placeholders or arithmetic mismatch.
- External formal amounts and documents require the deployment's confirmation policy.
