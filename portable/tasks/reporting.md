# Reporting Task

Report each material customer by verified name, country, stage, latest intent,
product/use/quantity/destination, action, verification, blocker, next owner and
due condition. Totals come only from the verified action ledger: inquiries,
unique WhatsApp/email customers, sent/delivered/read/replied/failed, CRM writes,
stage changes, refusal/archive/public-pool actions, quote/freight/PI queues and
account exceptions. Unknown is `未核验`, not zero.
