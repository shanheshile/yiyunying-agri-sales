# AI Effectiveness Measurement Task

Measure only repeated, verified real-business work. For each run capture a random
run ID, pseudonymous actor, task type, date, baseline minutes, AI execution,
preparation, human review, rework, allocated maintenance, quality pass, risk and
security incidents. Never store customer identity, content, credentials or CRM
record IDs. Compute net saved time after every human/maintenance cost, net
efficiency, annual net value, adoption and sustainability evidence. Require a baseline core
quality metric, risk metric, actual-user validation and reproducible artifact.
Unknown evidence stays unknown. Never decide an award, tier or payout; provide
the evidence to authorized reviewers. Keep collection
outside the operational prompt and evaluate in a separate daily/weekly run.
